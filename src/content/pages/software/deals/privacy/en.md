---
title: Privacy
description: What Deals stores and why.
---

Deals has no accounts. Here is everything it handles.

- **A buyer's request** (description, email and phone number) is stored on our servers so sellers can find it. The description is visible to anyone who searches. The email and phone number are shown only to the one seller who pays to unlock the request.
- **Retention:** unlocked requests are kept for two weeks as a backup, then deleted. Requests that nobody unlocks are deleted after 72 hours.
- **Search text** (a buyer's description or a seller's search) is sent to Vercel AI Gateway and Cohere, which turn it into a numerical form used for matching and rank the results. We don't store seller searches.
- **Your language choice, a seller's listings, saved searches and unlocked contacts** are stored only on your own device (in your browser, or in a local file in the command-line app).
- **Your IP address** is used to limit abuse. When a seller starts an unlock, it is stored with the request and deleted with it.
- **Payments** are processed by Stripe. We never see card details, only a confirmation that a payment was made.
- **Hosting** is provided by Cloudflare, which processes traffic for us.

We don't use analytics or tracking cookies. Other than the unlock described above, we don't sell or share your data. Stripe, Cloudflare, Vercel and Cohere have their own privacy policies.

The controller is [Metalogrom](https://metalogrom.com), contact: [help@metalogrom.com](mailto:help@metalogrom.com). To remove a request before it expires, write to us. Under the GDPR you can ask us about, correct or delete your data, and you can complain to your data protection authority (in Poland, UODO).
