---
title: Privacy
description: What Herds stores and why.
---

Herds has no accounts. Here is everything it handles.

- **Your name** is stored only in your browser. Other people in the room see it when you chat.
- **Your language choice** is stored in a cookie in your browser.
- **Messages** are shown to people in the room at that moment and are not stored.
- **Your IP address** is used while you are connected to limit abuse. If you are blocked by a moderator, the IP is stored until the block expires, then deleted.
- **Hosting** is provided by Cloudflare, which processes traffic for us.

We don't use analytics or tracking cookies, and we don't sell data. The news column may contain sponsored posts. We publish them ourselves and they don't track you. Links in them lead to other websites, which have their own privacy policies.

The controller is [Metalogrom](https://metalogrom.com), contact: [help@metalogrom.com](mailto:help@metalogrom.com). Under the GDPR you can ask us about, correct or delete your data, and you can complain to your data protection authority (in Poland, UODO).
