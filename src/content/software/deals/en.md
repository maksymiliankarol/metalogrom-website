Most deals start with someone looking for something. On Deals, the request comes first. Buyers describe what they need in their own words, and sellers search for requests that match what they offer, so their time goes to buyers who are already looking.

Posting a request is free and needs no account. Your email and phone number stay hidden until a seller pays a one-time fee to unlock them, and only one seller can unlock a given request. Please keep contact details out of the description.
