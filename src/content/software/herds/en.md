---
title: Herds
description: Chat with people in your city.
---

Every city has its own conversations: what's open, what's worth doing this weekend, what's going on around the corner. Herds puts them in one place. Search for your city, pick a room, choose a name and start talking with the people who live nearby. There is no account, email or password, and each room also has a short feed of local news.

Chats are live only and messages are not saved. Names are not reserved, so anyone can take yours once you leave. Please be kind: moderators can remove people from a room.

[Get Started](https://herds.metalogrom.com)
